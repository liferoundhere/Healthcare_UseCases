# Research Brief 08: The Cisco Healthcare Portfolio, Mapped to Hospital Use Cases, with Customer Evidence

Research date: Friday, November 13, 2026
Scope: Cisco's own 2026 healthcare positioning, a product-to-use-case map for the 10 storyboard use cases, the healthcare partner ecosystem, named customer stories from 2023 to 2026, the workplace side of a health system, and any public statement that ties Cloud Control or AgenticOps to healthcare.

## How to read this brief

- Bracketed numbers such as [4] refer to the numbered sources in section H.
- **VENDOR-REPORTED** marks a metric published by Cisco, Splunk, Webex, a partner, or the customer inside a vendor-published story. None of the customer metrics in this brief were independently audited.
- Quotes are exact, contiguous text from the source. Most pages were read through an automated fetch-and-extract tool that returns quotes of up to about 125 characters, so many quotes are short fragments of longer sentences. Quotes from the Dayton Children's, Maebashi, Central Health and State of Wireless PDFs were read directly from the PDF text.
- cisco.com customer-story pages carry no publication date. Where a story is undated, the table says so and gives whatever dating evidence the page itself contains.
- This brief does not repeat brief 05 (Cloud Control and Data Fabric status) or brief 04 section F (Cisco AI status table). For which products connect to Cloud Control today, use brief 05 section C.

## A) Executive summary

1. **Cisco names four healthcare solution areas, each with three use cases.** The Industry Use Case Explorer for healthcare lists "Clinical and operational workflows", "Healthcare infrastructure and operations", "Patient care anywhere" and "Security and trust", plus three priority topics: "AI-ready data centers", "Future-proofed workplaces" and "Digital resilience" [3]. The storyboard's 10 use cases map cleanly onto these 12 Cisco-named use cases (section B).
2. **The most current healthcare-specific Cisco document is a security brief.** "Modernizing Cybersecurity in Healthcare" was updated October 16, 2026. It names more than 40 products and services, ties them to HIPAA, HITECH, NIST CSF, NIST SP 800-207 and the HHS Cybersecurity Performance Goals, and lists "Security in Cisco Cloud Control (formerly Defense Orchestrator)" [4].
3. **Cisco maintains healthcare validated designs that were refreshed in 2026.** "Validated Profile: Healthcare (SD-Access) Vertical" was updated October 26, 2026 and was tested at 502 fabric sites, 5,000 devices and 100,000 endpoints [5]. "Validated Profile: Healthcare (SD-WAN)" was updated March 4, 2026 [6]. Two Epic validated designs exist (FlashStack, July 2023; FlexPod, November 2022) [7][8].
4. **The strongest ransomware-containment proof point is Dayton Children's (US).** With about 25,000 devices on the network, the hospital dropped a partner connection five minutes after an alert, and "In another five minutes, five MRI machines ... were automatically quarantined" using Ordr policies enforced on Cisco infrastructure. "Notably, every device that had Cisco Secure Endpoint installed was untouched." VENDOR-REPORTED [36].
5. **The strongest "AI at work" proof point is Community Health Network (US, August 2026).** Webex AI Agent integrated with Epic reached a 40% containment rate for appointment-related calls, with a 95% patient-matching success rate. VENDOR-REPORTED [22].
6. **The strongest EHR and imaging experience proof point is NYC Health + Hospitals (US).** CTO Jeffrey Lutz: "The full-stack observability we get with AppDynamics and ThousandEyes enables us to trace slowdowns to their source" and "The fact that our radiologists can reliably read and assess PACS imaging from any location is a game changer." No quantified metric is published [33].
7. **Medical device security is a partner story built on ISE.** Cisco's own partner pages cover Medigate (now Claroty) and Claroty with ISE [44][45]. Claroty lists integrations with ISE, Meraki, Cisco Spaces and Catalyst 9000 switches [46]. Ordr lists ISE, Meraki, Catalyst Center and firewall integrations [48]. Cisco also publishes ISE partner pages for Armis and Cynerio [65][66]. Dayton Children's is the named customer for ISE plus Ordr [36].
8. **Cloud Control and AgenticOps: no healthcare customer story and no healthcare SKU were found.** Three public touchpoints exist: the healthcare security brief lists "Security in Cisco Cloud Control" [4]; a Tech Field Day write-up of a June 2, 2026 Cisco Live session describes a policy change in "a simulated hospital environment" on a cloud-managed fabric [61]; and a June 2, 2026 Cisco blog mentions AI adoption "from office spaces to hospitals to factory floors" [62]. See section F.
9. **Gaps that matter for the storyboard.** No Cisco-branded "hospital-at-home" solution was found. No Cisco-native tap-and-go badge sign-on for shared clinical workstations was found. No Webex video-visit integration for Oracle Health was found. No named hospital running a Cisco AI POD for imaging inference was found. Details are in section I.
